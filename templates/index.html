How it works

  • MemoryRemembers facts you share and recalls them in later runs.
  • PerceptionTurns your intent into a goal list and tracks what is done.
  • DecisionChooses the next web search, fetch, file tool, or final answer.
  • ActionRuns that choice and returns results to the loop.
